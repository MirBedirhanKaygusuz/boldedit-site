BoldEdit

Turn one long video into a week of reels.

BoldEdit is an iOS app for creators. It cuts a long talking-head video into short vertical clips, adds captions automatically, and publishes those clips to your own TikTok and YouTube accounts on a schedule you choose — all edited on your iPhone.

Download on the App Store

What BoldEdit does

Everything you need to go from one recording to a week of short-form posts.

Cuts the dead air

Silences and filler words are removed automatically, so a rambling take becomes a tight clip.

Writes the captions

Speech-to-text runs on your iPhone and produces word-level captions you can restyle.

Finds the clips

One long recording is split into several short, self-contained vertical reels.

Schedules the posts

You pick a time; BoldEdit publishes the finished clip to the account you connected.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish, without leaving the app.

  1. Import your video

    Pick a long recording from your camera roll. Nothing is uploaded — it stays on your device.

  2. Auto-cut & caption

    BoldEdit trims silences, transcribes on-device, and lays down word-level captions you can style.

  3. Choose your clips

    Review the short vertical reels it found and fine-tune cuts, captions and framing.

  4. Schedule & publish

    Connect an account and post now or on a schedule, straight from your iPhone to the platform.

Inside the app

Editing, transcription and styling all happen on your iPhone.

Your footage stays on your iPhone

All editing and transcription run on the device. BoldEdit has no content server and no user accounts — your video is never uploaded to us. The only time a video leaves your iPhone is when you explicitly publish it, and then it goes straight from your device to the platform you chose.

Connecting your accounts

Connecting an account is optional and always starts from you, in Profile → Connected Accounts. You can disconnect at any time from the same screen.

YouTube

BoldEdit uses YouTube API Services to upload a finished clip to your own channel. It asks for two permissions only: permission to upload videos, and permission to read your channel's name so the app can show which channel a post will go to. BoldEdit never edits or deletes anything on your channel. Before each upload you choose the title, description, visibility and the “made for kids” declaration yourself.

Your use of YouTube through BoldEdit is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke BoldEdit's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok

BoldEdit uses TikTok's Content Posting API to publish a finished clip to your own TikTok account. You choose the caption, who can view the post, the interaction settings and any commercial-content disclosure before anything is sent. BoldEdit never reads or changes your existing TikTok content.

Pricing & requirements

BoldEdit runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is free to use, with an optional subscription that unlocks translation, Auto Clips and watermark-free exports. You can cancel anytime; subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID.

Frequently asked questions

Is BoldEdit free?

Yes. The core editor, on-device transcription and captions are free. An optional subscription unlocks translation, Auto Clips and watermark-free exports.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere but your device — until you publish. All cutting and transcription run locally on your iPhone. BoldEdit has no content server. When you publish, the clip goes directly from your phone to the platform you picked.

Which accounts can I connect?

TikTok and YouTube. Connecting is optional and starts from Profile → Connected Accounts. BoldEdit only ever posts to your own account, and only the posts you create.

Can I disconnect an account later?

Yes, at any time from the same Connected Accounts screen. You can also revoke access from the platform directly — for YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Do I need an account to use the editor?

No. BoldEdit has no sign-up. You can import, edit, caption and export without any account. Connecting TikTok or YouTube is only needed if you want to publish directly from the app.
