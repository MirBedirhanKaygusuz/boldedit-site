Features

Everything you need to go from one long recording to a week of short-form posts — edited entirely on your iPhone.

What BoldEdit does

Cuts the dead air

Silences and filler words are detected and removed automatically, so a rambling take becomes a tight, watchable clip. Every cut is shown on the timeline — keep or restore any of them.

Writes the captions

Speech-to-text runs on your iPhone and produces accurate, word-level captions. Nothing is sent to a server for transcription.

Finds the clips

One long recording is split into several short, self-contained vertical reels, so a single session becomes multiple posts.

Styles that fit your brand

Restyle captions — font, size, color and animation — and reuse a look across every clip for a consistent feed.

Translation & subtitles

Optionally translate captions to reach a wider audience, with timing kept in sync to the speech.

Schedules the posts

Pick a time and BoldEdit publishes the finished clip to the account you connected — post now or queue for later.

Inside the app

Editing, transcription and styling all happen on your iPhone.

Requirements & pricing

BoldEdit runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is free to use, with an optional subscription that unlocks translation, Auto Clips and watermark-free exports. You can cancel anytime; subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. See the Support page for details.
