Support

Answers to common questions, device requirements, and how to reach us.

Frequently asked questions

Is BoldEdit free?

Yes. The core editor, on-device transcription and captions are free. An optional subscription unlocks translation, Auto Clips and watermark-free exports. You can cancel anytime through your Apple ID.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere but your device — until you publish. All cutting and transcription run locally on your iPhone. BoldEdit has no content server. When you publish, the clip goes directly from your phone to the platform you picked. See the Publishing page.

Which accounts can I connect?

TikTok and YouTube. Connecting is optional and starts from Profile → Connected Accounts. BoldEdit only ever posts to your own account, and only the posts you create.

Can I disconnect an account later?

Yes, at any time from the same Connected Accounts screen. You can also revoke access from the platform directly — for YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Do I need an account to use the editor?

No. BoldEdit has no sign-up. You can import, edit, caption and export without any account. Connecting TikTok or YouTube is only needed if you want to publish directly from the app.

What languages are supported?

On-device transcription supports many languages, and captions can optionally be translated. Accuracy varies by language and recording quality.

Requirements & pricing

BoldEdit runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The app is free to use; the optional subscription unlocks translation, Auto Clips and watermark-free exports and is managed through your Apple ID.

Contact us
